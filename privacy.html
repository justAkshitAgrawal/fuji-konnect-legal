Fuji Konnect privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Fuji Konnect is an independent app. It is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by FUJIFILM. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

What stays on your phone

  • Safety backups of your camera's settings, your saved recipes, recipes you create, and your install history. These never leave your phone.
  • When you connect a camera over Bluetooth or a USB cable, the app reads and writes its settings directly. Nothing from your camera is sent to our servers.
  • Bluetooth pairing is handled by Android. The app does not read or use your location, even though older Android versions require the location permission to be declared for Bluetooth scanning.

What is sent to our servers

Only if you sign in:

  • Your Google account email and a user id, used to sign you in.
  • Your handle and optional display name, which are public.
  • Recipes you publish, with their tags and notes, and the photos you choose to attach, which are public.
  • Which recipes you liked and any reports you send, which are private.

Without an account, the app only reads the public recipe catalogue and sends an anonymous count when a recipe is installed.

Photos

The app only uploads photos you pick yourself when publishing a recipe. It does not read your photo library otherwise, and it never uploads photos from your camera.

What we do not collect

No advertising identifiers, no analytics, no precise or approximate location, no contacts, and no files from your camera or phone beyond the photos you choose to publish.

Third parties

Supabase hosts the catalogue, sign-in and photo storage. Google provides sign-in. Each has its own privacy policy. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Profile, Delete account removes your profile, published recipes, photos, likes and reports.
  • Without the app: email akshitaggarwal7@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and ask for your account to be deleted. We will delete it within 30 days.
  • Data stored only on your phone is removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change and the new version will be published at the same address.
